Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Lead Qualification Agent ("we", "us", or "our") handles information received through WhatsApp and other customer communication channels.

1. Information We Collect

We may receive information provided by customers during conversations, including name, phone number, email address, messages, product or service inquiries, quantities, and other information voluntarily provided by the customer.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to understand customer inquiries, qualify potential leads, identify products or services of interest, determine purchasing intent, respond to inquiries, and improve business communication.

3. AI Processing

Customer messages may be processed using automated systems and artificial intelligence to analyze inquiries and classify leads. Automated analysis may identify information such as product interest, quantity, urgency, buying signals, and missing information.

4. WhatsApp

Customer communications received through WhatsApp are processed for the purpose of handling and qualifying business inquiries. WhatsApp services are provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. and are subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the application. Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for business operations, lead management, and related purposes.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell customer personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the application, such as communication, hosting, database, and artificial intelligence services.

7. Customer Rights

Customers may request information about their personal data or request correction or deletion where applicable.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact us through the business communication channel associated with this application.
